1. Introduction

Welcome to NoteNest ("we," "our," or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how NoteNest handles user data.

NoteNest is designed as a 100% Offline-First, Local Note-Taking Mobile Application. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information or note content over the internet.

2. Information We Collect

NoteNest collects ZERO personal data off-device.

  • No User Account / Login Required: You do not need to register, create an account, or log in with email or social credentials to use the application.
  • No Remote Telemetry or Analytics: We do not integrate analytics tools (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Sentry).
  • No Advertising Tracking: We do not display advertisements and do not integrate advertising SDKs (e.g., Google AdMob).
  • No Third-Party AI API Data Transmission: All UI interactions, note categorization, and assistant formatting operate strictly on your local device. No note text is transmitted to external cloud LLM services or servers.

3. How Your Data is Stored

All notes, titles, tags, categories, app settings, and timestamps created within the app are stored strictly locally on your device inside the application's private database sandbox using local storage.

Your data remains entirely under your control on your physical hardware.

4. Third-Party Services & Links

NoteNest does not use third-party cloud backend services, external databases, or remote tracking services.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Deleting Individual Notes: You can delete any note or checklist directly within the app at any time.
  • Clearing All App Data: You can permanently delete all notes and configuration settings by navigating to your device's Android Settings > Apps > NoteNest > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling NoteNest immediately and permanently deletes all local database files from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

NoteNest is a general productivity application suitable for users of all ages. Because we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 in compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR regulations.

7. Security of Your Data

While no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, NoteNest relies on Android's operating system SELinux application sandbox to isolate your data from other applications on your device. We encourage users to maintain device lock patterns, PINs, or biometric security to protect physical access to their device.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect future app enhancements or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date."
