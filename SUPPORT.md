## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Does NoteNest AI require an internet connection?
**No.** NoteNest AI is a 100% offline-first application. All features, search functionality, category management, and formatting tools work without an active Wi-Fi or cellular internet connection.

### 2. Where are my notes stored?
Your notes are stored securely on your own device inside the application's local Hive database. Your content never leaves your physical phone.

### 3. Does NoteNest AI send my personal notes to cloud servers or AI services?
**No.** Zero bytes of personal note data leave your device. All note processing, formatting, and categorization operate locally on your hardware.

### 4. How can I back up my notes?
Since NoteNest AI does not use cloud accounts, you can back up your device storage using standard Android backup tools (Google One device backup / Android system backup) or export your notes using the in-app export feature.

### 5. How do I delete all my notes?
You can delete individual notes within the app, or clear all application data completely by going to **Android Settings > Apps > NoteNest AI > Storage > Clear Data**.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Guide

### Issue: The app is not showing recent notes after updating
- **Solution:** Force close the app and reopen it. NoteNest AI automatically verifies the local Hive database migration upon startup.

### Issue: Keyboard covers formatting options
- **Solution:** Ensure system navigation or virtual keyboard is set to standard height. NoteNest AI includes automatic `windowSoftInputMode="adjustResize"` to dynamically adjust toolbars above the keyboard.
