❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. Does NoteNest require an internet connection?

No. NoteNest is a 100% offline-first application. All features, search functionality, category management, and formatting tools work without an active Wi-Fi or cellular internet connection.

2. Where are my notes stored?

Your notes are stored securely on your own device inside the application's local database. Your content never leaves your physical phone.

3. Does NoteNest send my personal notes to cloud servers?

No. Zero bytes of personal note data leave your device. All note processing, formatting, and categorization operate locally on your hardware.

4. How can I back up my notes?

Since NoteNest does not use cloud accounts, you can back up your device storage using standard Android backup tools (Google One device backup / Android system backup) or export your notes using the in-app export feature.

5. How do I delete all my notes?

You can delete individual notes within the app, or clear all application data completely by going to Android Settings > Apps > NoteNest > Storage > Clear Data.

🛠️ Troubleshooting Guide

  • Issue: The app is not showing recent notes after updating
    Solution: Force close the app and reopen it. NoteNest automatically verifies local database migration upon startup.
  • Issue: Keyboard covers formatting options
    Solution: Ensure system navigation or virtual keyboard is set to standard height. NoteNest includes automatic windowSoftInputMode="adjustResize" to dynamically adjust toolbars above the keyboard.
